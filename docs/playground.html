Playground

Type a query. It is parsed, validated and matched against the record in your browser, by the same engine the Go module uses, compiled to WebAssembly. Nothing is sent to a server.

As you type, the engine suggests what can come next: fields, the operators a field allows, and the values a field lists. Label keys and the values of the other fields are taken from the record below, the way a host would fetch them from its own data. Use the arrow keys and Enter or Tab, or click one.
